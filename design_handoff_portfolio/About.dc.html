I’m Mike — an aspiring software engineer in San Diego who likes building things that feel warm, honest, and a little handmade.

I came to software the long way around: I’ve always been drawn to how things are made, whether that’s a well-shot roll of 35mm film, a record that sounds better on vinyl, or a piece of software that just quietly works. Somewhere along the way those interests collided, and I found I loved sitting at the seam between design and engineering.

These days I’m focused on front-end and full-stack work — shipping interfaces that are simple to understand and pleasant to sit in. I care a lot about the small stuff: the timing of an animation, the weight of a typeface, the feeling you get in the first three seconds of a page.

When I’m not at a keyboard

You’ll find me shooting film around San Diego, digging through record crates, or chasing a good sunset from the Bay to the beach. A lot of what I make online is just an attempt to bottle a little of that feeling.
